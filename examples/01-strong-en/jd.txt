Senior Backend Engineer — Payments Platform

We're hiring a Senior Backend Engineer to own services in our Payments Platform. You'll work on high-throughput services that move money between buyers, sellers and our settlement partners.

Responsibilities
- Design and ship Go services that handle payment authorisation, capture, refunds and reconciliation
- Own service reliability end-to-end: SLOs, on-call rotation, incident write-ups
- Partner with Risk and Compliance to roll out controls for AML / SCA / PCI scope
- Mentor 1-2 mid-level engineers on the team

Requirements
- 6+ years of backend engineering experience
- Strong Go (or willingness to ramp up fast from another typed language)
- Production experience with Postgres at scale: indexing, query planning, partitioning, schema migrations
- gRPC / protobuf service interfaces
- Hands-on experience operating services on Kubernetes
- Comfort with payments domain concepts: idempotency, retries, eventual consistency between ledger and processor

Nice to have
- Prior exposure to PCI-DSS scope or Open Banking APIs
- Experience designing for multi-region failover
- Reading/writing Portuguese or Spanish (most of our merchant base is in LATAM and Iberia)

Location: Remote (EU / LATAM time zones), or our Lisbon office.
