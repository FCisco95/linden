Product Manager — Developer Tools

Requirements

- Comfort writing specs and RFCs that engineers actually want to read
- Track record of shipping (open source, internal tooling, side projects count)
- Strong written communication
- Empathy for developer workflows — you know what a good error message looks like

Nice to have
- Prior IC engineering experience
- Open source maintenance
- Familiarity with our space (CI/CD, DevTools, observability)

Location: Remote.
